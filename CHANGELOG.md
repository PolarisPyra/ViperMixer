# Changelog

All notable changes to Viper Mixer are documented here, for the people who use it.
Each release adds its notes at the top; see docs/release.md.

## [26.11.0] - 2026-11-06

### Changed

- Viper Mixer has been rebuilt with a new look: five channel strips with hardware-style faders, a level readout in dB, a glowing mute button, and a new logo. Your assignments, levels and mute notes carry over from the previous version.
- A strip can now drive your output device or your microphone, and follows the default device when you switch it, such as plugging in headphones.

### Added

- Each strip can now learn its fader from your controller, and its mute from any button, not only ones that send notes: press the FADER or MUTE chip under a strip, then move a fader or press a button. Right-click a chip to unbind it.
- Mute buttons can follow a latching switch or flip on each press of a button that springs back; choose Latch or Toggle in the footer.
- Your MIDI controller is found again after you unplug it or plug it into another USB port, and the MIDI menu shows which device is connected.
